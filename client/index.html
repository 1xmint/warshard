FIELD GUIDE

Knowledge is your advantage.

WASD moves relative to facing. Left-drag looks around; right-drag turns your character; both mouse buttons run forward. Scroll to zoom. Tab cycles living enemies; click a character to select it. The first right-click captures the mouse for play. A game cursor appears between drags; Escape releases the mouse to the browser. Right-click again to resume capture. Keys 1–5 activate the current kit. Hover an ability for its range, cast time and effect.

For a ground ability, press its key then click the arena. Escape cancels placement. Moving cancels a cast. Stone pillars block movement and line of sight.

Build energy through combat. At 100%, press R for a 12-second transformation. Arcanist's Spellstorm speeds up casts and primes Sunshard; other heroes gain alternate abilities. Shields absorb damage; interrupts cancel casts and briefly lock their school.

Choose Arcanist duel for a solo practice opponent. Duel Lab changes difficulty, restarts the fight and controls sound. Flashkindle guarantees critical hits: two critical hits prepare an instant Sunshard. A round ends on defeat; choose Fight again to restart.
