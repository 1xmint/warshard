FIELD GUIDE

Knowledge is your advantage.

WASD moves. Tab cycles living enemies; click a character to select it. Keys 1–5 activate the current kit. Hover an ability for its range, cast time and effect.

For a ground ability, press its key then click the arena. Escape cancels placement. Moving cancels a cast. Stone pillars block movement and line of sight.

Build energy through combat. At 100%, press R for a 12-second transformation and five alternate abilities. Shields absorb damage; interrupts cancel casts and briefly lock their school.

Use two browser tabs in the same arena channel to duel. A round ends on defeat and restarts automatically. There is no audio in this slice.
