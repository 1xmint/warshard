# Warshard state

## Fire elemental rework — 2026-10-18 (approved, in progress)
Josh wants archetype-first fire gameplay: burns, spread, explosions and an Ascension that changes the rotation. The combined damage/slow/interrupt and generic root are rejected as the direction for this hero. PR #14 remains the running build; Josh approved the original fire elemental and eight-button layout. Implement the roadmap below; the new hero is not shipped yet.

Recommendation: an original small fire elemental, provisional identity Cinderborn, a mid-range damage dealer that builds pressure and converts it into timed explosions. Eight active keys (1–5/Q/E/R), removing this hero's F/C rather than filling ten slots. No ice-style slow/root or standalone interrupt. Preserve other heroes' control and the simulation's interrupt/DR systems. No damage pushback, mandatory dodging or precision aiming.

One short-loop resource replaces Kindling/Focus for this hero: three Ember pips. Mobile 1 builds one; direct criticals add an Ember, and two-charge instant 2 is a reliable critical converter usable during a cast. At three Embers, 3 releases instant Sunshard and spends them. Its critical can seed the next cycle. Stationary 4, Furnace Bolt, earns more Embers than mobile 1 and spreads a limited Ignite from a burning victim. Burn criticals can contribute at a shared per-caster interval; direct AoE generation is bounded per cast. R has a separate earned Ascension meter, bankable for a good opportunity rather than auto-transforming. No third heat/proc meter.

Baseline 1–5: mobile Cinder Dart, two-charge Flashkindle, Ember-spending Sunshard, committed Furnace Bolt, two-charge Cinderstep. Q Molten Shell absorbs damage; E Crownfall is a delayed meteor applying Ignite. 5/Q/E retain recognizable jobs across forms. F/C are unbound for this hero. Q does not grant interrupt immunity or solve cast pushback.

R Firestorm visibly transforms the elemental and replaces 1–4 for a bounded window: a fast building flame jet; a charged explosive burst; a miniature-sun finisher that converts a bounded portion of remaining Ignite into immediate explosion damage; and a temporary Living Sun that echoes the next few finishers. Echoes cannot produce Embers/Ascension, trigger further echoes or recursively spread. Detonation spends the converted burn portion, never duplicates its entire remaining damage. Keep one summon with finite echoes; no pet bar or permanent companion. Exact timings, damage and progression rate remain tuning assumptions.

PvP flow: probe/ignite → earn a safe cast or teammate setup → force a defense → choose whether to commit Ascension → capitalize or retreat/rebuild. Opponents can use cover, defensive cooldowns, separation and pressure during committed casts; the hero must not need freeze/slow/interrupt bundled into every attack. Test 1v1 and team play separately: neither implies the other is balanced.

Implementation roadmap after the product decision: (1) prove the base Ember loop with deterministic scenarios and solo play, preserving existing modes; (2) implement the genuinely different Ascension kit and bounded summon/detonation contracts; (3) teach fair bots to execute and counter it, give each action distinct original/redistributable visuals, and validate browser flows on CI. Keep current Rust/TypeScript/Babylon boundaries. Tests stay on CI; local compilation/lint only. Prove inputs, resource conservation, interruption/cancellation, no recursive gains, reset/recovery and form expiry; subjective fun needs Josh's playtest.

Research: Blizzard's [cooldown trading guidance](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/21464978/opening-moves-balancing-the-cooldown-equation) and [positioning/CC guidance](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/21952448/midgame-moves-for-lights-sake-stay-alive) support the PvP flow, not a current meta prescription. Its [historical Fire design explanation](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/19941177) stresses player control of proc conversion; [June 2026 tuning](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24276957) distinguishes steady casting pressure from burst. Official [Human Torch](https://www.marvelrivals.com/20250220/41360_1213050.html) and [Hulk](https://www.marvelrivals.com/20241123/41360_1195678.html) kits demonstrate effect interactions and transformations. These are design references, not copied abilities/assets or evidence that our proposal is fun/balanced.

## Expanded Arcanist kit (2026-10-18, superseded design)
Josh expanded the active scope to ten keys and a connected mobile-burst playstyle. Roadmap: (1) preserve bot navigation/team/GCD corrections; (2) implement authoritative mobile casts, explicit off-GCD rules, delayed area impact and radial roots with regression scenarios; (3) connect the ten-key HUD, bot priorities and spell presentation, then validate on CI and review screenshots. Use existing Rust/TypeScript/Babylon boundaries; no new services or progression.
Kit: 1 Cinder Dart, 1.0s moving cast, 50 damage, primes Kindling on a normal hit (cannot complete it without a crit); 2 Flare Lance, 1.5s stationary filler, 145 damage; 3 Flashkindle, two guaranteed-crit instant charges, 55 base damage, sequential 10s recharge; 4 Sunshard, 4.5s/instant proc, 300 damage, crits feed Kindling; 5 Riftstep, two 5m blink charges/sequential 12s recharge, off-GCD and cancels your cast. Q Prism Ward, 240 shield/6s, 20s cooldown, off-GCD and preserves a cast. E Crownfall, 0.75s stationary cast then 1.2s fall onto the target's captured position, 240 base damage in 2.7m with criticals feeding Kindling, 20s cooldown. R Firestorm retains the 12s transformation and launches a 180-base-damage/4m explosion that applies 22-base-damage Ignite each second for 8s; burn criticals advance Kindling at most once per second across all targets. F Sunderflare deals 70 damage, slows 1.5s and interrupts/locks school for 3s/10s cooldown. C Glasswake roots enemies within 2.7m for 3s/18s cooldown using existing root diminishing returns.
Utility slots are Q=5, E=6, F=7, C=8; R remains an independent ultimate. Off-GCD behavior belongs to ability definitions rather than slot numbers. Only movement, blink and interrupt cancel ordinary casts; the shield does not. Meteor aim locks when its cast completes; its area appears during descent, damages only enemies still in range/LOS at impact and is cleared on reset. Damage causes no cast pushback; Q absorbs damage rather than granting interrupt immunity. Every key needs a distinct animation/effect and tactical role. Firestorm uses an optional data-defined form activation; burns refresh per source/target/ability, never stack, follow their victim, and clear on death/reset/match end. Initial numbers are tuning values; fun/balance require playtests.
